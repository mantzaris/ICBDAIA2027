\begin{abstract}
We test bounded reinspection of underwater acoustic waveforms using linked spectrograms, numerical proposals, component memory, and a vision-language controller. Five policies share one backbone; classification and localization provide separate comparisons. The inventory contains 210 real, 35 mixed, and 35 synthetic scenes. Both AI annotation checkpoints failed morphology qualification, making primary real agreement unavailable. On twelve exact synthetic test cases, adaptive F achieved event F1 0.511 versus fixed G3's 0.478 and heuristic G4's 0.642. All 570 main episodes executed, but schema-invalid and empty reports remained. These controls do not establish adaptive benefit or real physical accuracy. \pending{Secondary results.}
\keywords{Underwater acoustics \and Spectrogram interpretation \and Tool-using agents}
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and approach}
LOFAR/DEMON fusion supports frequency estimation~\cite{li2022lofar} and classification~\cite{najamuddin2026fusion}; vision-language models also interpret marine-mammal spectrograms~\cite{nihal2025knowledge}. \emph{From Signals to Evidence} produces structured simulated-vessel descriptions~\cite{zhang2026evidence1} and multichannel detections~\cite{li2026evidence2}. We test bounded reinspection; fusion and evidence reports are established. ReAct provides the decision/action pattern~\cite{yao2023react}; UA-Bench motivates synthetic and silent-input controls~\cite{sun2026uabench}.

\textbf{Measurements.} LOFAR (low-frequency analysis and recording) uses a Hann, one-sided, power-normalized STFT: uncalibrated digital power/Hz. Global windows/hops are 1/0.25\,s over 0--1800\,Hz after anti-aliased resampling to at most 4\,kHz; inspections offer 0.25/1/4\,s windows. DEMON (detection of envelope modulation on noise) analyzes the filtered analytic envelope, with 4/0.5\,s windows/hops and 0.5--200\,Hz modulation. Carriers are initially 500--2000\,Hz, additionally 2000--5000/5000--8000\,Hz when sampling/filter margins permit. Both views retain original time; neither identifies a source.

CUDA measurements propose tones, chirps, transients, broadband regions, and modulation. Memory retains trajectories, resolution, carriers, and evidence IDs; intersecting boxes alone do not merge crossing ridges. Graph edges retain temporal overlap and unresolved associations. Numerical checks require cited measurements, matching morphology/domain/carrier, endpoints within two native bins, time boundaries, and ordered tracks. Claims cite retained components, but prose is not semantically verified. Raw reports/rejections survive; retention means measurement consistency.

\textbf{Policies and comparisons.}\label{sec:controllers}
Pinned Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct~\cite{qwen3vl} uses bfloat16 and greedy decoding: 160 selection tokens, 2048 report tokens, and six components. JSON grammar constrains syntax, not correctness. G1 reads global LOFAR; G2 reads paired views and measurements; G3 orders inspections; G4 prioritizes unseen tools/carriers and unresolved relations; F generates selections from the valid menu. G2--G4/F share proposals and checking. Labels/simulator parameters are hidden. Six slots count invalid/unavailable calls and finalization; tools inspect, measure, track, test harmonics/overlap, or compare intervals.

Ablations remove DEMON, tables, graph, LOFAR-resolution adaptation, or numerical filtering, enforce exclusivity, or randomize actions. Removing tables retains graph values/axes; DEMON remains 4\,s. F without numerical filtering retains schema checks. Separate P1/P2 LOFAR/dual-view CNNs train on weak labels. Localizers use 192/48 exact synthetic training/validation scenes (including broadband bursts) and validation thresholds under six components. Both comparisons use three seeds. Models are not parameter-matched. P1's shared preparation computes DEMON; its timing is not standalone LOFAR cost. Training details are recorded in the package.

\textbf{AI reference protocol.}\label{sec:references}
A pre-test amendment replaced human annotation with blinded InternVL 3.5 4B/8B~\cite{wang2025internvl35}. Four fixed views are prompted separately, with disjoint training demonstrations and a full 1\,Hz DEMON mean-PSD grid. Method outputs/labels are hidden; shared InternVL/Qwen ancestry permits correlated errors. Qualification requires at least three gold events and matches per morphology/domain, precision/recall $\geq0.5$, and $\geq80\%$ valid relevant views. Fixed 4\,Hz matching cannot be relaxed by model bins; rejected typed rows count against precision.

Pilots exposed all twelve calibration controls; later interface trials reused two, and the final prompt added demonstrations. Calibration is development evidence. Twelve separate controls audit the frozen protocol without retuning. Neither checkpoint recovered chirps/modulation in calibration or audit; transient/broadband had only two/zero gold events and could not qualify. The freeze admits no morphology (Table~\ref{tab:annotation}).

\section{Evaluation and execution}
Primary real AI-reference agreement and H1 (F versus validation-selected G3/G4) and H4 (raw-to-filtered agreement/coverage) are unavailable; no comparator is selected. Exact synthetic recovery is separate. Greedy one-to-one matching requires morphology/domain agreement, temporal IoU $\geq0.3$, and track error within 2\,Hz; regions require time--frequency IoU $\geq0.3$ and modulation requires compatible carriers. Duplicates stay unmatched. Modulation truth is exhaustive only for amplitude-modulated noise, beating, mixed, and noise/silence cases; compatible higher beat harmonics are optional. Training/test seeds are disjoint.

Bootstrap units are recording/day, connected-mixture, or generator-case groups, not model seeds. Planned H1/H4 use Holm correction. After qualification failed but before generative main outcomes, an amendment applied existing ablations to twelve synthetic cases, separately from the original 80 real scenes. Budget runs use six real/twelve synthetic cases at two/four/six slots. Manifests define 54 interventions and thirty duration, view-mismatch, attenuation, and removal probes. These are secondary; waveform changes regenerate views, while image mismatch retains target numerical evidence. Blank-input success requires valid empty reports; contrast ranking is uncalibrated.

DSP, synthesis, model forward/backpropagation, and metrics use a 48\,GB RTX 6000 Ada. Pinned image preprocessing runs on CPU, contrary to plan; frozen settings remain unchanged. Mixed batches divide CUDA spans equally; untimed verification can overlap another span. Accounting is incomplete/nonexclusive, not standalone latency or marginal/billable cost. Wall time includes preparation/verification/waiting. Failures remain in denominators; defective pilots supply historical timing only.

\section{Results and limitations}
All 570 main episodes executed without execution failures. Exact-synthetic F1 for G1/G2/G3/G4/F was 0.024/0.529/0.478/0.642/0.511; F's 95\% generator-case interval was [0.286,0.700]. These descriptive comparisons neither select a post-test primary comparator nor establish adaptive benefit or equivalence. Synthetic G2/F had two/one schema failures; real G3/G4 had two each. Real empty reports numbered 14/43/46/42/39, respectively; F returned 14/30 DeepShip and 25/60 MBARI empty. Emission is not calibrated abstention or accuracy.

F's synthetic TP/FP/FN changed from raw 13/32/11 to retained 12/11/12. The lost match came from an entire schema-invalid report: this combines schema gating and numerical filtering. Real mean recorded spans were F 125.2\,s versus G3/G4 24.0/27.4\,s; F wall median/p95 were 1403/1779\,s, under the accounting limits above.

P1/P2 present-class macro F1 was $0.350\pm0.210$/$0.245\pm0.007$ (three-seed mean$\pm$SD); four-label F1 was 0.262/0.184. Paired four-label P2-minus-P1 was $-0.078$ (recording-group 95\% interval $[-0.135,0.052]$, conditional on seeds). Nine test recordings, with no Tug, establish neither dual-view superiority nor four-class generalization. Synthetic localizer F1 was 0.143/0.165/0.163 (P1) and 0.161/0.193/0.190 (P2), seeds 17/29/43; malformed P1 seed-43 track output was rejected.
