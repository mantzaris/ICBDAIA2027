\begin{abstract}
A persistent acoustic line and a periodic envelope need not identify a common physical source. We study bounded reinspection using deterministic measurements, an overlap-preserving graph, generative action selection, and numerical report checks. Five controllers share a local vision-language backbone; supervised classifiers and synthetic-trained localizers provide separate comparisons. The inventory contains 210 real scenes from two domains, 35 real mixtures, and 35 synthetic scenes. Both candidate AI annotation models failed the declared morphology qualification gate: each matched only two of fifteen controlled tonal events. Consequently, primary real-scene agreement is unavailable, rather than zero accuracy. \section{From recording labels to localized evidence}
A vessel label does not identify which line persists, which transient overlaps it, or whether periodic structures share a source. We compare additional waveform inspections against fixed and deterministic policies using the same report model. Low-frequency analysis and recording (LOFAR) and detection of envelope modulation on noise (DEMON) supply complementary displays. A DEMON peak indicates periodic envelope structure within its carrier band, not blade count or propulsion mechanism. The contribution is an auditable comparison of inspection, component provenance, and filtering. Observable morphology, internal consistency, and physical identification remain distinct.

\section{Related work}
Li et al. combine LOFAR and DEMON for target counting and fundamental-frequency estimation~\cite{li2022lofar}; Najamuddin et al. combine machinery and propeller features for classification~\cite{najamuddin2026fusion}. Dual-view fusion is therefore an established comparison.

Nihal et al. study knowledge-augmented vision-language interpretation of marine-mammal spectrograms~\cite{nihal2025knowledge}. Zhang et al. couple complementary acoustic representations to a language model for structured descriptions of simulated vessel signals~\cite{zhang2026evidence1}. The companion passive-detection study combines multichannel evidence and identifies field validation as future work~\cite{li2026evidence2}. Evidence-bearing acoustic reports precede this study; our narrower question concerns additional waveform inspections and unresolved associations.

Interleaved decisions and external actions follow ReAct~\cite{yao2023react}. UA-Bench motivates synthetic and silent-audio controls~\cite{sun2026uabench}. These controls and AI-reference agreement do not establish real physical correctness.

\section{Measurements, memory, and report checking}
\subsection{Linked views retain distinct frequency meanings}
For a sampled observation $x[n]$, a Hann window $w[n]$ of length $N$, and hop $H$,
\begin{equation}
X[m,k]=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x[n+mH]w[n]e^{-2\pi\mathrm{i}kn/N},
\qquad
P[m,k]=\frac{c_k|X[m,k]|^2}{f_s\sum_nw[n]^2}.
\end{equation}
The one-sided factor $c_k$ is two at interior positive frequencies and one at DC/Nyquist. Frame centers retain original coordinates. PSD uses uncalibrated digital amplitude squared per hertz; bin spacing does not guarantee component resolution.

Global LOFAR uses a 1\,s window and 0.25\,s hop over 0--1800\,Hz after anti-aliased resampling to at most 4\,kHz; inspections offer 0.25, 1, and 4\,s windows. For carrier-filtered $u_b$, the envelope $e_b=|u_b+\mathrm{i}\mathcal H\{u_b\}|$ is filtered, resampled, and analyzed with a 4\,s window and 0.5\,s hop. DEMON displays modulation frequencies of 0.5--200\,Hz with a separate carrier label. Duration, carrier margins, and edge guards determine availability.

Deterministic CUDA measurements propose tone, chirp, transient, broadband, and modulation components from unpooled PSD; learned localizers are separate baselines. Display pooling does not determine numeric resolution. The linked views are correlated transforms of one waveform.

\section{Discussion and limitations}
The failed reference gate prevents plausible AI labels from becoming assumed truth. Increasing checkpoint size did not recover the missing calibration morphologies, but the small, development-exposed controls cannot establish universal model inability. The verifier may also reject useful findings missed by its proposals. Mono ambiguity, weak labels, incomplete grouping, the small DeepShip subset, domain shift, and one frozen backbone limit generalization. MBARI adds a recording domain, not vessel-class truth. \pending{Conclude from completed controlled comparisons.} Execution success and synthetic recovery cannot replace independent real accuracy.
