About ICBDAIA’27

The University of National and World Economy (UNWE), Sofia, Bulgaria, and Ibn Tofail University, Kingdom of Morocco, in official collaboration with the STUDIES-DIG project team funded under the Horizon Europe programme, are organizing the 6th International Conference on Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Applications (ICBDAIA'27). The conference will be held at the University of National and World Economy (UNWE), Sofia, Bulgaria, on 21–22 April 2027.
 

The 6th Edition of the Springer ICBDAIA'27 builds upon the success of its previous Springer proceedings, held in Valencia, Spain (June 2026), Kénitra, Morocco (May 2023 and July 2025), and Laayoune, Morocco (November 2023 and November 2024). These successful editions have firmly established ICBDAIA as a recognized international forum for presenting high-quality research, promoting scientific collaboration, and fostering innovation in the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, Data Science, and their emerging applications.

ICBDAIA'27 will bring together leading researchers, academics, scientists, engineers, industry professionals, innovators, and practitioners from around the world to exchange ideas, present cutting-edge research, and discuss the latest advances in Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Intelligent Systems, the Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud and Edge Computing, Digital Transformation, Generative AI, and Large Language Models (LLMs). The conference also welcomes interdisciplinary contributions highlighting innovative applications of these technologies in healthcare, agriculture, education, economics, finance, smart cities, energy, transportation, environmental sustainability, industry, and public administration.
