\begin{abstract}
We test bounded reinspection of underwater acoustic waveforms using linked spectrograms, numerical proposals, component memory, and a vision-language controller. Five policies share one backbone; classification and localization provide separate comparisons. The inventory contains 210 real, 35 mixed, and 35 synthetic scenes. Both AI annotation checkpoints failed morphology qualification, making primary real agreement unavailable. On twelve exact synthetic test cases, adaptive F achieved event F1 0.511 versus fixed G3's 0.478 and heuristic G4's 0.642. All 570 main episodes executed, but schema-invalid and empty reports remained. Larger inspection budgets did not consistently improve recovery. These controls do not establish adaptive benefit or real physical accuracy.
\keywords{Underwater acoustics \and Spectrogram interpretation \and Tool-using agents}
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and approach}
LOFAR/DEMON fusion supports frequency estimation~\cite{li2022lofar} and classification~\cite{najamuddin2026fusion}; vision-language models interpret marine-mammal spectrograms~\cite{nihal2025knowledge}. \emph{From Signals to Evidence} produces simulated-vessel descriptions~\cite{zhang2026evidence1} and multichannel detections~\cite{li2026evidence2}. We test bounded reinspection using ReAct's decision/action pattern~\cite{yao2023react} and synthetic/silent controls motivated by UA-Bench~\cite{sun2026uabench}.

\textbf{Measurements.} LOFAR (low-frequency analysis and recording) uses a Hann, one-sided, power-normalized STFT in uncalibrated digital power/Hz. Global windows/hops are 1/0.25\,s over 0--1800\,Hz after anti-aliased resampling to at most 4\,kHz; inspections offer 0.25/1/4\,s windows. DEMON (detection of envelope modulation on noise) analyzes the filtered analytic envelope with 4/0.5\,s windows/hops over 0.5--200\,Hz. Carriers are 500--2000\,Hz, plus 2000--5000/5000--8000\,Hz when available. Views retain original time, without source identification.

CUDA measurements propose tones, chirps, transients, broadband regions, and modulation. Graph memory retains trajectories, resolution, carriers, evidence IDs, temporal overlap, and unresolved associations; intersecting boxes alone do not merge ridges. Checks require cited measurements, matching morphology/domain/carrier, endpoints within two native bins, time boundaries, and ordered tracks. Retention means measurement consistency; prose is not semantically verified. Raw reports/rejections survive.

\textbf{Policies and comparisons.}\label{sec:controllers}
Pinned Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct~\cite{qwen3vl} uses bfloat16 and greedy decoding: 160 selection tokens, 2048 report tokens, and six components. JSON grammar constrains syntax, not correctness. G1 reads global LOFAR; G2 reads paired views and measurements; G3 orders inspections; G4 prioritizes unseen tools/carriers and unresolved relations; F selects from the valid menu. G2--G4/F share proposals/checking. Labels/simulator parameters are hidden. Six slots count invalid/unavailable calls and finalization.

Ablations remove DEMON, tables, graph, LOFAR-resolution adaptation, or numerical filtering, enforce exclusivity, or randomize actions. Removing tables retains graph values/axes; DEMON remains 4\,s. Removing numerical filtering retains schema checks. Separate P1/P2 LOFAR/dual-view CNNs train on weak labels. Localizers use 192/48 exact synthetic training/validation scenes, including broadband bursts, and validation thresholds under six components. Both use three seeds. Models are not parameter-matched; P1's shared preparation computes unconsumed DEMON. The package records training details.

\section{Data and reference feasibility}
The 210 intact 30\,s excerpts comprise 150 DeepShip~\cite{deepshipdata} (90/30/30 train/validation/test) and 60 test-only MBARI days~\cite{mbari}, with offsets/hashes. Real splits contain 26/9/69 recording/day groups. Missing vessel/session IDs preclude vessel-disjoint claims; MBARI lacks class truth. Accessible QiandaoEar22 clips lacked proven continuity~\cite{du2024qiandao}. A bounded 13,500-pair similarity screen found no cross-split candidates, without ruling out shared sources.

Another 35 real mixtures and 35 synthetic scenes each split 17/6/12; thus the main cohort has 90 real, twelve mixed, and twelve synthetic cases. Intact recordings are 75\% of inventory. Mixtures span 30--60\,s, five overlap fractions (0--100\%), and $-10/0/+10$\,dB power ratios. Test mixtures form one connected provenance group; sparse cells do not support replicated-factorial inference. Parent timing is not component truth.

\textbf{AI reference protocol.}\label{sec:references}
A pre-test amendment replaced human annotation with blinded InternVL 3.5 4B/8B~\cite{wang2025internvl35}. Four fixed views are prompted separately, with disjoint training demonstrations and a full 1\,Hz DEMON mean-PSD grid. Method outputs/labels are hidden; shared InternVL/Qwen ancestry permits correlated errors. Qualification requires at least three gold events and matches per morphology/domain, precision/recall $\geq0.5$, and $\geq80\%$ valid relevant views. Fixed 4\,Hz matching cannot be relaxed by model bins; rejected typed rows count against precision.

Pilots exposed all twelve calibration controls; later interface trials reused two, and the final prompt added demonstrations. Calibration is development evidence. Twelve separate controls audit the frozen protocol without retuning. Neither checkpoint recovered chirps/modulation in calibration or audit; transient/broadband had only two/zero gold events and could not qualify. The freeze admits no morphology (Table~\ref{tab:annotation}).

\section{Evaluation and execution}
Primary real AI-reference agreement and H1 (F versus validation-selected G3/G4) and H4 (raw-to-filtered agreement/coverage) are unavailable; no comparator is selected. Exact synthetic recovery is separate. Greedy one-to-one matching requires morphology/domain agreement, temporal IoU $\geq0.3$, and track error within 2\,Hz; regions require time--frequency IoU $\geq0.3$ and modulation requires compatible carriers. Duplicates stay unmatched. Modulation truth is exhaustive only for amplitude-modulated noise, beating, mixed, and noise/silence cases; compatible higher beat harmonics are optional. Training/test seeds are disjoint. Secondary relation scores distinguish emitted temporal-overlap edges from geometry-induced time--frequency intersections using 2\,Hz narrowband tubes.

Bootstrap units are recording/day, connected-mixture, or generator-case groups. Planned H1/H4 use Holm correction. A user runtime cap during ablations after main scoring reduced 80 real scenes to the first 33 frozen scenes (30 DeepShip, three MBARI; twelve groups): 198 episodes across six variants, without execution failures. This change was not preregistered and is domain-imbalanced. All 72 new synthetic-variant episodes were omitted; F/G4/F without numerical filtering reuse main generations. Budgets pair six real and twelve synthetic cases. Secondary tests comprise 54 interventions and thirty sensitivity probes. Waveform edits regenerate views; image mismatch retains target numerical evidence. Blank success requires valid empty reports; contrast ranking is uncalibrated.

DSP, synthesis, model forward/backpropagation, and metrics use a 48\,GB RTX 6000 Ada. Pinned image preprocessing runs on CPU, contrary to plan. Shared batches divide CUDA spans equally; verification shares the lock but lacks a timer. These incomplete, schedule-dependent spans do not measure standalone latency or marginal/billable cost. Wall time includes preparation/verification/waiting. Failures remain in denominators; defective pilots supply historical timing only.

\section{Results and limitations}
All 570 main episodes executed without execution failures. Exact-synthetic F1 for G1/G2/G3/G4/F was 0.024/0.529/0.478/0.642/0.511; F's 95\% generator-case interval was [0.286,0.700]. These descriptive results establish neither adaptive benefit nor equivalence. Synthetic G2/F had two/one schema failures; real G3/G4 had two each. Real empty reports numbered 14/43/46/42/39; F returned 14/30 DeepShip and 25/60 MBARI empty. Emission is not calibrated abstention or accuracy.

F's synthetic TP/FP/FN changed from raw 13/32/11 to retained 12/11/12. The lost match came from an entire schema-invalid report: this combines schema gating and numerical filtering. On the matched six-real-scene subset at six slots, mean recorded spans were F/G3/G4 113.0/22.9/32.0\,s, under the accounting limits above.

\researchfigure{figure06.pdf}{Separate real report production, exact synthetic controller/localizer recovery, and weak-label classification. Primary real agreement is unavailable.}{fig:quantitative}

P1/P2 present-class macro F1 was $0.350\pm0.210$/$0.245\pm0.007$ (three-seed mean$\pm$SD); four-label F1 was 0.262/0.184. Paired four-label P2-minus-P1 was $-0.078$ (recording-group 95\% interval $[-0.135,0.052]$, conditional on seeds). Nine test recordings, with no Tug, establish neither dual-view superiority nor four-class generalization. Synthetic localizer F1 was $0.157\pm0.012$/$0.181\pm0.018$ (seeds 17/29/43); malformed P1 seed-43 track output was rejected.

\researchfigure{figure07.pdf}{Post-main user cap: real variants and a separate three-policy synthetic reuse comparison. No new synthetic variants ran; F without numerical filtering retains schema checks.}{fig:ablation}

Graph/table removal yielded 33/33 empty retained real reports versus F's 16/33. Emptiness includes rejection; real accuracy remains unavailable. On twelve reused synthetic cases, F without numerical filtering had TP/FP/FN 12/27/12 (F1 0.381), versus F's 12/11/12 (0.511), with the same schema gate and generations.

Budget F1 (2/4/6 slots) on the same twelve controls was F 0.613/0.613/0.511, G3 0.613/0.586/0.478, and G4 0.556/0.455/0.642: no consistent gain, with schema failures included.

Across 54 interventions/six base cases, pooled F1 was F/G4 0.489/0.534; the different main cohort precludes pooled degradation estimates. Repeated blank waveforms yielded valid empty reports, without independent success estimates. Nonblank zero-support recovery and real risk--coverage remain unavailable.

F's tone/chirp/transient/modulation F1 was 0.485/0.400/0.667/0.800 on 15/4/2/3 gold events; broadband had no positives. Emitted temporal-edge F1 was F/G4 0.077/0.375; both missed the only gold time--frequency-overlap edge. Free-text reasoning was unscored.

View-mismatch report agreement was F/G4 0.947/1.000 (five scored pairs each; one both-empty pair omitted). This measures stability, not correctness or visual use. Real attenuation/removal probes lack component truth.
